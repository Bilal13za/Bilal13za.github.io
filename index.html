About

I'm pursuing Chartered Accountancy through ICAP, currently at the Certificate in Accounting and Finance (CAF) stage. My passed papers cover financial accounting and reporting, cost and management accounting, business law, and managerial and financial analysis; I'm now working through taxation, business insights and analysis, and audit.

What sets my study apart is that I build my own instruments. Every tool on this page started as a gap I hit while studying — planning audit procedures from risk factors, tracing a tax treatment from transaction to return impact, structuring a business case — and became a deterministic, offline application with its reasoning fully traceable. No black boxes: every output links back to the rule, source, or input that produced it.

Beyond the syllabus, I'm developing an interest in corporate finance, valuation, and capital markets, and in how disciplined analysis supports better business decisions. My goal is to secure articleship at a reputable audit firm in Pakistan and build toward qualification.
