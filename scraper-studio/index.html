Scraper Studio Web

Turn any market-data page into a clean spreadsheet — no installs, works offline after first load.

1Get the page

Browsers can't fetch other sites directly, so bring the page to the app: open the market-data page, press Ctrl+U (or type view-source: before the address on your phone), copy everything, paste below. Or load a saved .html file.

Where do I get market-data pages?

Any PSX/broker page with a prices table works. The app never sends the page anywhere — everything happens on your device. Tip: pages that load data with JavaScript may show empty tables in view-source; in that case use your browser's "Save page" first, then load the saved file.
